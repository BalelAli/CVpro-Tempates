How to build a strong resume

Start with a clear professional summary, then add relevant skills, education and experience. If your work history is limited, use projects, coursework, volunteering and measurable achievements to show what you can do.

Keep it easy to scan

Use standard section headings, concise bullet points and job-related keywords. Avoid unnecessary graphics when applying through an applicant tracking system, and customize your resume for each position.

Before you apply

Check spelling, contact details, dates and formatting. Save a clean final copy and make sure the most relevant information appears near the top.
